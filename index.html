About Tom Holland

Tom Holland is a British actor and producer, best known for his role as Spider-Man in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. In the summer of 2026, Tom Holland starred as the lead in two of the biggest box office hits in Hollywood history. One was his fourth solo movie, Spider-Man: Brand New Day, which surpassed $2.4 billion worldwide to become the highest-grossing Spider-Man film of all time. The other was director Christopher Nolan's epic masterpiece, Odyssey.
